## Calibration implications

- **Retain snapshot epsilon 0.02.** Both full scans had zero snapshot drift. This run offers no evidence that increasing the epsilon would help. Meaningful-ingest drift still needs a later measurement.
- **Retain keyword Jaccard minimum 0.2.** The keyword arm was never the sole admitter in the executed set; changing its value would not remove these admitted pairs. This does not establish what a lower threshold would admit.
- **Leave verdict hysteresis unimplemented pending follow-up.** Before execution, 24 of 161 inconsistent readings were novel with max neighbour score in [0.55, 0.60). After execution, 10 of the 21 residual inconsistent readings fall in that band. The residual is now enriched near the proposed band (47.6%), warranting follow-up, but this backfill-dominated run and its two previously judged targets do not establish repeated flip-back churn or justify automatically suppressing those flags.

No constants were changed. Schedule or manually run the next measurement at least a week after this completed baseline (September 11 or later), or after meaningful ingestion. No automation was created. The results were subsequently posted to #174; it remains open for follow-up.
